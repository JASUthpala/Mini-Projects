Lecture AI

Lecture AI is a smart local assistant that helps you turn lecture materials into quick, useful study insights. It can process lecture PDFs and audio recordings, extract the content, summarize the main ideas, and generate practice questions to help you revise faster.

What it does

  • Reads PDF lecture notes automatically
  • Transcribes audio files with Whisper
  • Creates a short summary of the topic
  • Generates study questions for revision

How to use it

  • Choose a lecture file in PDF, MP3, or WAV format
  • Click the upload button
  • Wait while the local AI processes your lecture
  • Review the summary and generated questions
